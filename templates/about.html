About MedIntel AI

Empowering African Healthcare Innovation

Bridging the gap between groundbreaking medical products and complex regulatory compliance across Nigeria and Africa.

Our Mission

Incomplete dossiers and regulatory ambiguity are the primary causes of registration delays for medical devices and pharmaceuticals in Nigeria. MedIntel AI was built to solve this information bottleneck by providing instant, grounded, and verifiable regulatory intelligence.

We empower clinical engineers, founders, manufacturers, and regulatory affairs professionals to navigate NAFDAC, WHO, and international standards with precision and confidence.

Key Capabilities

Intel Chat (RAG)

Answers grounded exclusively in official NAFDAC, FDA, and WHO directives with direct source citations.

Device Classifier

AI-assisted risk classification (Class A–D) aligned with NAFDAC 2025 regulations & IMDRF SaMD rules.

Compliance Workflows

Step-by-step registration checklists for drug products, medical devices, IVDs, and WHO GMP compliance.

Verifiable Grounding

Safety-first AI architecture that refuses to hallucinate requirements and links directly to reference documents.

Important Regulatory Disclaimer

MedIntel AI provides regulatory intelligence and workflow support for informational purposes only. It does not constitute legal or formal regulatory advice. Always confirm final requirements directly with NAFDAC or relevant authorized bodies.
